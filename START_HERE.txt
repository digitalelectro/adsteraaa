==============================
ZERO-CODING QUICK START (COPY/PASTE)
==============================

1) Download this folder (Git clone or Download ZIP) and open the "adsteraaa" folder.

2) Put your Vertex service-account key file anywhere on your computer. Remember the full path (for example, C:\Users\You\Downloads\vertex-key.json or /Users/you/Downloads/vertex-key.json).

3) The download does NOT include a .env file. In the adsteraaa folder, make a copy of .env.example named .env (or just rename .env.example to .env) and fill in three lines (keep it in the same folder as app.py):
   PROJECT_ID=your-gcp-project-id
   LOCATION=your-vertex-region (for example: us-central1)
   GOOGLE_APPLICATION_CREDENTIALS=/full/path/to/your/vertex-key.json

4) Start the app:
   - On Windows: double-click scripts/run_local.bat (Command Prompt will open and install/run automatically).
   - On Mac/Linux: open Terminal, run: bash scripts/run_local.sh

5) Wait until Streamlit prints a local URL (usually http://localhost:8501). Open that link in your browser.

6) In the sidebar, confirm Project ID, Region, and Key Path are filled. Add your global image style (e.g., "cinematic, detailed, 16:9").

7) Upload your timestamped JSON lines and click the big buttons in order: "Build segments and generate prompts" then "Generate images with Vertex."

If anything fails, recheck the path to your key file and that your service account has the Vertex AI User role.
